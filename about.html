Junior Front-End Developer / Web Designer

Ridgewood, NY (NYC)

I'm interested in front-end development because I like the mix of structure, visuals, and problem solving. I enjoy building websites that feel clean, easy to follow, and thoughtfully put together.

I also bring a photographer's eye to layout and visuals. A lot of how I think about spacing, composition, image choice, and balance carries over into the way I design and build pages.

Right now I'm looking for junior front-end and web design opportunities where I can keep improving, contribute to real projects, and continue developing both the technical and visual side of my work.
