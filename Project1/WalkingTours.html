Explore on Foot

New Orleans is best experienced by strolling through its historic streets. Join a guided walking tour of the French Quarter, admire the stunning architecture of the Garden District, and soak up the city's lively atmosphere along the Mississippi River.

Popular walking tour spots include:

  • French Quarter
  • Royal Street
  • Jackson Square
