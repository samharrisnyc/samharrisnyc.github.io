Why get a NYC Library Card?

Free Access to Books and eBooks:
- Borrow physical books, audiobooks, and eBooks for free.
- Access platforms like Libby, OverDrive, and SimplyE for digital content.

Streaming Services:
- Free streaming movies and documentaries through Kanopy.
- Access to streaming music and audiobooks.

Digital Magazines and Newspapers:
- Free digital access to newspapers (e.g., New York Times) and magazines.

Technology and Tools:
- Free computer use, Wi-Fi, and printing services (limited free printing).
- Availability of laptops and tablets to borrow for temporary use in select locations.

Museum and Cultural Passes:
- Reserve free passes to local museums and cultural institutions (Culture Pass program).

Workshops and Classes:
- Attend free workshops, classes, events, and lectures on various topics, including career building, technology, arts, and language learning.

Career and Job Resources:
- Support services for job seekers, including résumé building, job fairs, career coaching, and skills training.

Accessibility and Inclusion Services:
- Specialized services and accommodations for patrons with disabilities.
